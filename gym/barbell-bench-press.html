🏋️ Exercise Overview

The barbell bench press is a compound upper-body exercise primarily targeting the chest, triceps and front shoulders.

  • Primary muscle: Pectorals (chest)
  • Secondary muscles: Triceps and anterior deltoids
  • Equipment: Barbell and flat bench
  • Typical sets: 3–4
  • Typical reps: 8–12

📋 How to Perform the Exercise

1. Set Up

Lie flat on the bench with your eyes positioned approximately underneath the bar.

  • Keep your feet flat on the floor.
  • Maintain three points of contact: head, upper back and glutes on the bench.
  • Pull your shoulder blades back and down.
  • Maintain a comfortable, natural arch in your lower back.

2. Grip the Bar

Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.

  • Wrap your thumbs around the bar.
  • Keep wrists straight and stacked over forearms.
  • Position your hands evenly on the bar.

3. Unrack the Bar

Take a breath, brace your torso and lift the bar out of the rack.

  • Extend your arms without losing shoulder position.
  • Position the bar above your mid-chest.
  • Ask a spotter for assistance if needed.

4. Lower the Bar

Slowly lower the bar towards your mid-to-lower chest.

  • Keep elbows at approximately 45–75 degrees from your torso.
  • Keep wrists aligned over your elbows.
  • Control the movement throughout.
  • Touch the chest gently without bouncing.

5. Press the Bar

Push the bar upwards until your arms are extended.

  • Drive your feet into the floor.
  • Keep your upper back stable against the bench.
  • Press upwards and slightly backwards towards the rack.
  • Exhale as you press.
  • Avoid aggressively locking your elbows.

6. Re-rack

Once your set is complete, carefully guide the bar back into the rack hooks.

Make sure both ends are securely supported before releasing your grip.

⭐ Best Practices

  • Start light: Learn the movement with an empty bar before adding weight.
  • Control the bar: Avoid bouncing or dropping the bar onto your chest.
  • Maintain stability: Keep feet planted and shoulder blades retracted.
  • Use consistent technique: Maintain the same range of motion for every repetition.
  • Breathe properly: Brace before lowering and exhale during the press.
  • Progress gradually: Increase weight only when you can maintain good form.
  • Warm up: Perform lighter warm-up sets before working sets.
Technique tip: Think about pulling the bar down towards your chest rather than simply letting gravity lower it. This encourages a controlled and stable movement.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Flaring elbows excessively.
  • Allowing wrists to bend backwards.
  • Bouncing the bar off the chest.
  • Lifting the glutes off the bench.
  • Using excessive weight at the expense of technique.
  • Allowing shoulders to roll forwards.
  • Using an incomplete or inconsistent range of motion.

🛡️ Safety Guidelines

Important: Use a competent spotter or correctly positioned safety arms when bench pressing without a spotter. Set the rack height and safety catches before beginning your set.
  • Never use a weight you cannot control.
  • Always use a closed grip with thumbs around the bar.
  • Check that weight plates are secured.
  • Stop if you experience sharp pain or unusual discomfort.
  • Do not attempt maximal lifts without appropriate supervision and safety equipment.
